select a map
Drag an agent or ability straight from the right-hand panel onto the map — or click one to arm it, then click the map. Select tool: drag to move, Delete removes.
Areas are drawn at true in-game scale. Radial abilities are fixed and cannot be resized. Lines and walls are aimed by dragging the handle; length only changes for abilities the game actually lets you vary (Fault Line's charge, Viper's wall), and never beyond their real maximum. Walls that are steered in the game (Harbor's, Phoenix's) are steered here too: drag out from the agent and the wall follows your hand, rounding off any corner tighter than the game can turn and stopping at the game's length. Let go early to stop it short.
